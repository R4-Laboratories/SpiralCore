## Execution and comparison binding

The workflow checks out the exact baseline
`4b8cb79fe5fca9e9527a2923924c887d9e7ddbf8` separately, verifies HEAD/clean state,
and builds only its gateway service binary. It copies that executable before the
candidate build can replace the shared target path. Both use pinned Rust 1.97.1,
release, locked dependencies, two jobs and no incremental compilation. Relevant
ambient compiler overrides are refused. Whole encoder, prefix/probe, dictionary
profile and both lockfiles must match byte-for-byte. Builds finish before traffic.

The harness selects the baseline executable for both endpoints of Combined-before
and the candidate executable for both endpoints of Combined-after and Whole.
Per-session role/hash metadata, before/after executable manifests, frozen input
checks, and separate source manifests retain that binding. Different executable
hashes are a sanity gate, not by themselves proof of a behavioral difference.

Fixture group JSON is identical between the two Combined arms; only the arm's
directory/binary selection differs. Existing deterministic fixture identities are
preserved. All arms have the same packet impairment, authenticated transport,
durability and readback rules. Fresh stores isolate arms, while both tiers share
their own persistent connection. Three complete arm-order rotations are enforced.

## Parity, accounting and boundaries

The registered arithmetic is consistent: 18 sessions, 216 deliveries, 264
operations (216 sends, 24 checkpoints, 24 schemas), and 1,717,047,288 requested
bytes. Both setup publications are charged once per Combined group. The analyzer
retains distinct group and connection-prefix totals rather than resetting or
duplicating the connection charge between tiers.

Six before/after pairs must match every operation's identity/length, mode,
publication path, frame/data byte counts, request/batch counts, and actual
bidirectional logical deltas. Null/missing fields are refused. The analyzer
recomputes parity from raw client receipts and matches the retained harness
receipt; it does not merely trust the campaign's success flag. Canonical Offer
and Data-body equality remains a separate frozen-baseline core test, accurately
distinguished from the network's identity/count parity.

Existing transfer-ID receiver joins, counter continuity, reopened object sets,
capture completion, calibration and queue checks remain active. Process CPU is
summed per trial; nested stage CPU is separate. Each comparison uses exactly
three matched trial ratios before its median. Zero denominators remain null.
Different captured IP totals from QUIC acknowledgments/retransmissions are
measured outcomes and are not incorrectly forced to be identical.

No new host routes, firewall/NAT changes, credential export, or external traffic
destination was introduced. Routing/forwarding and owned-process cleanup remain
inside the fresh namespaces. Services drop privileges after namespace entry.
Synthetic private keys/configs/large fixtures remain under the owned private
temporary directory; public receipts and frozen binaries are retained separately.
Workflow permissions remain read-only, same-repository PRs only, and checkout
credentials are not persisted. The older long Combined campaign now requires
its explicit benchmark label.

## Evidence and remaining qualification

Inspected retained successful receipts: fixture tests (five), analyzer tests
(eight), both focused Clippy exits, and pure infrastructure checks including
baseline/binary/order mutations and five parity negatives. Historical Runtime
and Combined analyses were regenerated byte-identically according to their
retained `analyzer_exit=0 cmp_exit=0` receipts. No such checks are represented as
network performance evidence.

Two non-blocking documentation leftovers were reported to root: registration
calibration prose still said four arms, and the analysis-method introduction
named older Runtime/Warm activity gates. Executable gates correctly use three
arms and the two Combined profiles. These descriptions can be corrected without
changing implementation or expanding testing.

Actual campaign execution, calibrated results, artifact custody and final source/
executable identities remain separate qualification steps. The experiment is
local shaped QUIC, not Internet-WAN, transparent TUN, MVM, or energy qualification.
